After analysis

Make a safe handoff.

  1. Observe from a safe distance and note the animal's exact location.
  2. Contact a trusted local animal NGO, veterinarian, or animal-control service.
  3. Share a clear photo and describe what you observed. Let a trained responder guide next steps.

Emergency guidance

Keep yourself and the animal safe.

If the animal is bleeding heavily, struggling to breathe, unconscious, or in immediate danger, contact your local emergency veterinarian or animal rescue service now. Do not approach, move, feed, or medicate an injured animal unless a professional tells you to.

All listed partners and prepared alerts are fictional school-project data. No real messages are sent.

About StrayAid AI

The problem

People who find an animal in distress may not know how to assess the situation or quickly find nearby help.

Our solution

StrayAid AI combines image classification with location and rescue information to support a quicker, informed handoff.

Our aim

We aim to help communities identify possible animal health concerns and connect with local rescue support.

Technology Used

  • HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • Google Teachable Machine
  • AI/ML image classification
  • Location and maps features
